# Aurora File Manager Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 3, 2026**

Aurora File Manager is designed to let you browse and manage files on your Android device. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

## Information and access

Aurora accesses files and directories on your device so it can display them to you and provide file-management features. This file access is the purpose of the app; file contents and listings are not collected or transmitted by Aurora to the developer or to third parties.

The app may access device diagnostics needed for its features to operate properly. These diagnostics are used for app functionality and are not collected or transmitted as user data.

## Root-level access

If you grant root-level access, Aurora can access files that are otherwise restricted by Android. This may include sensitive personal files and important system files. Aurora uses this access to display and manage files at your direction. Be cautious: changing or deleting the wrong file can cause data loss, serious system damage, or prevent your device from starting.

## Sharing and third parties

Aurora does not collect or transmit user data, and does not share user data with third parties.

## Your choices

You control the permissions you grant to Aurora, including any root authorization. You can revoke permissions through Android settings or your root-management app. Some features may not work without the permissions they require.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Aurora's data practices or features change. Updates will be published in this repository with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue in the Aurora File Manager GitHub repository.
